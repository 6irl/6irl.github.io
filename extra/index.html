Sensory and Abstract Names

Our names are meaningful to us and they carry all sorts of meaning especially subjective and personal meanings and they are usually written down or spoken. But what if you connect to a meaning, feeling or an abstract concept that is more difficult to write down or speak and you want that as your name? You could have a symbol which could be written or drawn or shown but that is much more difficult to speak, you could have a musical melodic name which could be played or sung but that is much more difficult to write down although there are ways. But they can be conveyed. But what if you have a connection with a feeling and you want that as a name? This could be touch, taste, smell, an emotion or set of emotions and an abstract thought or concept. All names have meaning and they can be deeply held by us. Some can be used practically and others it is more difficult but they are still valuable.

I have already recorded and conveyed my names that rely on sight/seeing/looking and sound/hearing/listening and these are my written mames, my symbolic name and my melodic names.

The following are my names which are sensory and/or abstract and I will attempt to convey them in words:

Touch: The feeling of damp dewey moss beneath my bare feet on a warm spring day when grounding. The feeling of my partners lips against my lips and the feeling of our hands holding and our cuddles.

Taste: The taste of chocolate.

Smell: The smell when I walk past a bakery that is baking fresh bread and pastries. The smell of sweet flower scent wafting on a warm summer breeze. The smell of freshly cut grass.

Emotion: That sense of childhood wonder, possibility, potential, curiosity and excitement. The feeling of anticipation when watching a gripping movie and of the cosiness of the sofa, a warm blanket and snacks and friends. LOVE!

Abstract Thought: That fleeting spark of inspiration. Déjà vu. Premonition.

Of course there is crossover of all these and senses as they operate in unison and symbiosis and this is experience.

We can have whatever names we want and can authentically express this for ourselves.
